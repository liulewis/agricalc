Seed Spacing Calculator

Find the exact in-row spacing needed to hit your target plant population.

Enter target plant population (plants per acre) and row spacing. The calculator computes the distance between seeds in the row (inches and cm), plants per foot of row, and total plants across your field — the numbers you need to set the planter.

A planter was set for 7-inch spacing at 30-inch rows but the seed meter was worn. Emergence showed 22,000 plants/acre instead of 32,000. Working backward from this calculator, the correct spacing for 32,000 at 30-inch rows is 6.6 inches — a 0.4-inch change worth 10,000 plants.

How the Calculation Works

Field area in square inches = acres × 43,560 × 144. Total row length = area ÷ row spacing. In-row spacing (in) = 6,272,640 ÷ (row spacing in × target plants per acre) (6,272,640 = 43,560 × 144). Plants per foot = 12 ÷ spacing. Total plants = target population × acres.

Real-World Example

30,000 plants/acre at 30-inch rows: spacing = 6,272,640 ÷ (30 × 30,000) = 6.97 in ≈ 17.7 cm. Plants per foot = 12 ÷ 6.97 ≈ 1.72. For 100 acres: 3.0 million plants.

Common Mistakes & Tips

Common mistakes: confusing row spacing with in-row spacing; ignoring germination and emergence loss (add 5–10% to seeding rate); using a worn seed meter; mixing acres with hectares. Calibrate the planter with the calculated spacing.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I set my planter from this spacing?

Mark the calculated distance on a measured length of row, lower the planter, and count seeds dropped. Adjust the meter plate or gearbox until the count matches. Check 3–4 times across the field.

Why add extra seeds for germination?

Field emergence is rarely 100%. If germination is 90% and emergence 85%, only ~77% of planted seeds become plants. Set the target 10–15% higher than your desired final population.

How does row spacing affect yield?

Narrower rows often capture more light and yield 5–10% more, but at the same population they require tighter in-row spacing. The calculator keeps population constant while changing spacing.

What if my rows are in metric?

Enter row spacing in inches and read the cm result, or convert: 1 inch = 2.54 cm. For metric population (plants/ha), divide by 2.471 to get plants/acre.

What is a good target population?

Corn: 28,000–36,000 plants/acre (higher for irrigation, lower for dryland). Soybeans: 120,000–160,000 plants/acre. Wheat: drilled, target 25–35 plants/sq ft.
