Formula & How It Works

N uptake ≈ 15 kg N per 1000 kg cherry produced

P₂O₅ uptake ≈ 3 kg P₂O₅ per 1000 kg cherry

K₂O uptake ≈ 18 kg K₂O per 1000 kg cherry

Soil test adjustment: if soil nutrients are low, add full recommendation; if adequate, reduce by 30-50%.

Urea = N needed ÷ 0.46 (urea is 46% N)

DAP = P needed ÷ 0.46 (DAP is 46% P₂O₅; also contributes 18% N)

MOP = K needed ÷ 0.60 (Muriate of Potash is 60% K₂O)

Calculation Example

5-year-old coffee at 3000 trees/ha, target 8,000 kg cherry/ha: N = 8 × 15 = 120 kg N/ha. P₂O₅ = 8 × 3 = 24 kg P₂O₅/ha. K₂O = 8 × 18 = 144 kg K₂O/ha. Urea = (120 − DAP contribution) ÷ 0.46 ≈ ~240 kg urea/ha. DAP = 24 ÷ 0.46 ≈ 52 kg DAP/ha. MOP = 144 ÷ 0.60 = 240 kg MOP/ha. Cost ≈ 240×0.50 + 52×0.60 + 240×0.55 = $287/ha.

Typical Fertilization Schedule

Year 1-2 (establishment): 50-100 g/tree split into 3-4 applications. Year 3-4 (early production): 100-200 g/tree. Year 5+ (full production): 200-400 g/tree, split into pre-bloom, main fill, and post-harvest. Apply in bands around drip line, not against trunk.

About This Tool

Coffee (Coffea arabica and C. canephora) is a high-value tropical crop. Mature arabica plantations produce 5-10 t cherry/ha; robusta 8-15 t/ha. Nutrient demand is heavy — N and K are the main drivers, with P critical for root development. This calculator follows FAO Coffee Nutrition Guidelines and ICRAF recommendations. Best management: split applications, soil testing every 2-3 years, and use of organic mulch/shade to reduce leaching.

Tips & Common Mistakes

Applying too much N causes excessive vegetative growth and lower bean quality. Not splitting K applications leads to leaching. Forgetting micronutrients (B, Zn, Mg) — coffee is sensitive to boron deficiency. Always soil test; do not rely on universal formulas alone. Young trees need less but more frequent applications.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much fertilizer does a coffee tree need? ▼
A mature coffee tree in full production needs 200-400 g of NPK blend per year, split into 3-4 applications. Young trees (1-3 years) need 50-150 g/year. Total nutrients: ~150 kg N, 30 kg P₂O₅, 180 kg K₂O per hectare per year for 10 t/ha cherry yield.Calculation guide →
When is the best time to fertilize coffee? ▼
Three key windows: (1) pre-bloom 4-6 weeks before flowering (N + K), (2) fruit set and fill 8-10 weeks after bloom (NPK balanced), (3) post-harvest recovery (K + P). Avoid applying during drought or just before heavy rain to prevent leaching.
Arabica vs Robusta fertilizer needs? ▼
Arabica (Coffea arabica) prefers higher elevations (800-2000 m), slightly acidic pH (6.0-6.5), and moderate N. Robusta (C. canephora) is more tolerant of lower elevations and hotter climates, responds well to higher N rates, and produces higher yields but at lower bean prices.
How do soil test results affect fertilizer rates? ▼
If soil test shows nutrients in the "low" range, apply the full recommended rate. If "medium," apply 70% of recommended. If "high," apply 30-50%. Always adjust for soil pH — coffee needs pH 5.5-6.5; below 5.0, apply lime first.
How do I use the Coffee Fertilizer Calculator correctly? ▼
Enter your actual planting density (trees per hectare). Use a soil test for N/P/K levels — if no test, use default values. Set target yield based on your farm's historical average, not the maximum possible. Enter local fertilizer prices to get cost estimates. Review recommendations against your local extension service.
