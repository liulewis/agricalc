How to Use This Calculator

Enter the soil organic carbon percentage from your lab report (if you have OM%, multiply by 0.58 to get SOC), bulk density, sampling depth and field area. Click Calculate to see the SOC stock and its CO₂ equivalent.

About This Tool

This calculator uses the standard IPCC soil carbon stock equation: SOC (t/ha) = SOC% × bulk density (g/cm³) × depth (cm) × 0.1. CO₂ equivalent uses the molecular weight ratio CO₂/C = 44/12 ≈ 3.667.

Applicability: Designed for temperate and subtropical soils, applicable to farming systems across North America and Europe.

How the Calculation Works

SOC stock = SOC% × BD × depth × 0.1. CO₂e = SOC × 3.667. Per-acre value (short tons C/acre) = t C/ha × 0.446. Total on field = SOC stock × field area.

Real-World Example

SOC = 2.0%, BD = 1.3 g/cm³, depth = 30 cm. SOC = 2.0 × 1.3 × 30 × 0.1 = 7.8 t C/ha. CO₂e = 7.8 × 3.667 = 28.6 t CO₂e/ha. On 100 ha, total C = 780 t, CO₂e = 2,860 t.

Common Mistakes & Tips

Common mistakes: confusing OM% with SOC% (multiply OM by 0.58), not adjusting bulk density for depth, sampling only the top 15 cm when reporting to 1 m, and treating surface SOC gains as permanent. SOC changes slowly; re-sample every 3–5 years using the same depth and method.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is soil organic carbon (SOC)?

Soil organic carbon is the carbon fraction of organic matter in soil (plant residues, microbes, humus). It is about 58% of total soil organic matter. SOC is the largest terrestrial carbon pool and the basis of carbon farming programs.

How do I convert OM% to SOC%?

Multiply organic matter percentage by 0.58 (Van Bemmelen factor). If your soil test reports 3% OM, SOC is about 1.74%. Conversely, SOC% × 1.724 ≈ OM%.

Why is the CO₂ multiplier 3.667?

Carbon dioxide has molecular weight 44 (C=12, O₂=32). One ton of carbon equals 44/12 = 3.667 tons of CO₂. This is the standard factor used in carbon accounting and greenhouse gas inventories.

How much carbon can a farm sequester?

Most cropland can sequester 0.2–1.0 t CO₂e/ha/yr with cover crops, reduced tillage and diversified rotations. Soils already high in SOC sequester less. Over 20 years, a well-managed farm may add 5–20 t CO₂e/ha.

How deep should I sample for carbon accounting?

IPCC and carbon standard protocols use 0–30 cm for Tier 1 reporting, but many programs require 0–10, 10–30 and 30–100 cm layers. Always sample to the same depth as your baseline and report bulk density alongside SOC.
