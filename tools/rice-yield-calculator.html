Rice Yield Calculator (Tropical & Temperate)

Estimate paddy rice yield using the standard yield components method: panicles per m², grains per panicle, fertility rate, and 1000-grain weight. Used by IRRI and national rice research programs.

Enter panicle density, grains per panicle, spikelet fertility, and 1000-grain weight to estimate rice yield in tons per hectare and bushels per acre.

Formula & How It Works

Filled Grains/m² = Panicles/m² × Grains/Panicle × Fertility%

Yield (t/ha) = (Filled Grains/m² × 1000-Grain Weight g) ÷ 1,000,000

(1000-grain weight in grams; filled grains per m²; result in metric tons per hectare)

Yield (bu/acre) = Yield (t/ha) × 14.87 (rough rice conversion)

Calculation Example

Irrigated rice: 300 panicles/m² × 120 grains × 85% fertility = 30,600 filled grains/m². At 25g 1000-grain weight: Yield = (30,600 × 25) ÷ 1,000,000 = 0.765 kg/m² = 7.65 t/ha = 113.8 bu/acre. For 1 ha: 7.65 tons.

How to Use This Calculator

1. Count panicles in 5-10 random 1m² quadrats at maturity. 2. Count grains on 10-20 representative panicles and average. 3. Estimate fertility (% filled vs empty spikelets). 4. Weigh 1000 grains (or use 20-28g typical for indica, 25-32g for japonica). Calculate.

About This Tool

Rice yield components are the standard method used by IRRI (International Rice Research Institute) and national programs for yield estimation before harvest. Typical yields: irrigated lowland 6-10 t/ha, rainfed lowland 2-4 t/ha, upland 1-3 t/ha. The Mekong Delta and Punjab achieve 8-10 t/ha; West African smallholders average 2-3 t/ha.

Common Mistakes & Tips

Counting panicles too early (before tillering stabilizes). Not accounting for sterile spikelets (fertility varies 60-95%). Using wrong 1000-grain weight (indica vs japonica differ). Forgetting moisture correction — yield is usually reported at 14% moisture.

Frequently Asked Questions

Typical rice yield components? ▼
Irrigated indica: 250-400 panicles/m², 100-150 grains/panicle, 80-95% fertility, 20-28g 1000-grain weight. Upland: 150-250 panicles/m², 60-100 grains, 60-80% fertility. Japonica: fewer panicles, more grains, heavier grains.
What is the yield gap in West Africa? ▼
Nigeria average ~2 t/ha vs potential 6-8 t/ha. Constraints: low fertilizer use (10-20 kg/ha vs recommended 80-120), drought, poor soils, weeds (especially Striga), and lack of improved varieties. IRRI and AfricaRice are developing NERICA varieties for upland conditions.
How does this compare to harvest yield? ▼
Component estimates are usually within 10-15% of actual harvest yield. Field losses (shattering, lodging, combine losses) reduce actual yield by 5-15%. Moisture content at harvest affects measured weight — standardize to 14% moisture for comparison.
What units does the Rice Yield Calculator use? ▼
Panicles per m² unitless, grains per panicle unitless, fertility rate in %, 1000-grain weight in grams, area in hectares. Results in t/ha, bu/acre, and total tons.
How do I use the Rice Yield Calculator correctly? ▼
Start by gathering accurate input values from your farm records or measurements. Enter each value in the specified units, then click Calculate. Review all results carefully — cross-check the primary output against your own experience or extension recommendations. For best results, use actual measured values rather than estimates, and recalibrate whenever conditions change.
