Formula & How It Works

Total Gain (lb) = Final Weight − Initial Weight

ADG (lb/day) = Total Gain ÷ Days

Feed:Gain Ratio = Total Feed Intake ÷ Total Gain

Calculation Example

Cattle from 500 to 800 lb in 120 days, eating 3,000 lb feed: Gain = 300 lb. ADG = 300÷120 = 2.5 lb/day. F:G = 3000÷300 = 10:1. Feed cost at $0.10/lb = $300.

How to Use This Calculator

1. Weigh animals at start and end. 2. Record days on feed. 3. Track total feed consumed. Calculate.

About This Tool

ADG and feed conversion are key profitability metrics in livestock production. Lower F:G ratios mean more efficient conversion of feed to saleable weight.

Common Mistakes & Tips

Not accounting for shrink (animals lose 2-5% weight in transit). Using inconsistent weighing times (full vs empty gut). Confusing F:G with G:F (inverse).

Frequently Asked Questions

Good ADG for feedlot cattle? ▼
2.5-4.0 lb/day typical, depending on genetics, diet, and implant program. Backgrounding/stocker: 1.5-2.5 lb/day.
Good feed conversion for cattle? ▼
Feedlot: 5.5-7.5 lb feed/lb gain (high-concentrate). Backgrounding: 8-12:1. Pigs: 2.5-3.0:1. Poultry: 1.6-2.0:1.
How to improve feed efficiency? ▼
Balanced rations (protein/energy), good feed quality, health management (parasites, disease), proper bunk management, and genetics.
What units does the Weight Gain Calculator use? ▼
Initial/final weight in lb, days on feed unitless, total feed intake in lb. Results in total gain lb, ADG lb/day, feed:gain ratio, and feed cost $.
How do I use the Weight Gain Calculator correctly? ▼
Start by gathering accurate input values from your farm records or measurements. Enter each value in the specified units, then click Calculate. Review all results carefully — cross-check the primary output against your own experience or extension recommendations. For best results, use actual measured values rather than estimates, and recalibrate whenever conditions change.
