REAP Final Rule: 5 Days Left to Comment — How to Be Heard

28 Oct 2026 Agricalc Editorial Team 4 min read

Related Calculators: agri subsidy calculator · farm ROI & payback calculator · equipment cost calculator

The Clock Is Running: Comments Close November 2

USDA's public comment window on the REAP final rule closes in five days, on November 2, 2026. After that, the docket (RBS-26-Business-0529) closes and the rule — 12-month wait to apply, no ground-mount solar on certified cropland, no Chinese-made components — moves forward on the administrative record as it stands. If the rule affects your farm or business, this is the last guaranteed chance to get your concerns on the record.

Why Your Comment Matters

Three reasons. First, USDA is legally required to review and respond to substantive comments; a thin record makes the rule easier to defend, a thick one harder. Second, the rule is already facing a court challenge (filed September 28, 2026, still pending) — comments become part of the administrative record the court examines. Third, comments have changed USDA programs before: specific, data-backed objections are the ones that move agencies.

How to Submit Your Comment in 10 Minutes

  • Step 1 — Go to regulations.gov and search for docket RBS-26-Business-0529 (the REAP final rule).
  • Step 2 — Write your comment. Say who you are (farmer, rural business owner, installer), how the rule affects you in concrete terms — project size, dollars at stake, jobs — and what you want changed. One page of specifics beats ten pages of generalities.
  • Step 3 — Submit before November 2. Online submission is instant; do not wait until the last evening in case the site is slow.

What Makes a Comment Effective

Be specific and be honest. Numbers carry weight: the size of your planned array, the grant amount you counted on, the payback period with and without REAP. Describe real consequences — a canceled project, a lost installer contract, a barn roof that will stay empty. Form letters count less than individual stories. And keep it factual: agencies discount comments they cannot verify.

What Happens After November 2

USDA reviews the comments and may adjust the rule, though major reversals are rare without court action. The lawsuit continues on its own track — litigation timelines are uncertain, so do not plan around a quick reversal. And watch for USDA's announcement of the first application window under the new rules; none has been announced yet.

Conclusion

Five days, one docket, ten minutes of your time. Whether you support or oppose the REAP changes, the comment window closing November 2 is the formal way to be heard. After that, the record is set — make sure your experience is part of it.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where do I submit my comment?

On regulations.gov — search docket RBS-26-Business-0529 (the REAP final rule) and use the comment form. Submit before November 2, 2026.

Does it cost anything to comment?

No. Submitting a public comment on a federal rule is free.

Can I comment if I'm not a farmer?

Yes. Anyone affected — installers, lenders, rural businesses, equipment dealers — can comment. The docket is public.

What if I miss the deadline?

Late comments are generally not considered. The court challenge continues independently, but the administrative comment record closes November 2.
