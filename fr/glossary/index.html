Glossaire Agricole

Glossaire Agricole

53+ termes et définitions agricoles.

CEC

Cation Exchange Capacity — a measure of soil's ability to hold and release nutrients.

pH

A measure of soil acidity or alkalinity, ranging from 0-14.

N-P-K

Nitrogen, Phosphorus, Potassium — the three primary macronutrients.

Field Capacity

The maximum amount of water soil can hold after drainage.

Evapotranspiration

The combined loss of water from soil evaporation and plant transpiration.

FCR

Feed Conversion Ratio — how efficiently animals convert feed into body mass.

GDD

Growing Degree Days — a measure of heat accumulation for crop development.

MRL

Maximum Residue Limit — the maximum allowed pesticide residue in food.

Substrate

The material in which mushrooms or plants grow.

Tilth

The physical condition of soil regarding itsuitability for planting.
