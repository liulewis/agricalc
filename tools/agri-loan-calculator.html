How the Calculation Works

This calculator uses the standard amortizing loan formula. Equal payment (amortizing): monthly payment = P × r × (1+r)^n / ((1+r)^n − 1), where P = loan principal, r = monthly interest rate (annual rate ÷ 12), n = number of monthly payments (years × 12). Equal principal: monthly principal repayment = P/n; total interest = (n+1) × P × r / 2. The preset FSA rates are the October 2026 farm loan rates published by the Farm Service Agency; they are updated monthly, and you can always adjust the rate manually.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate the profitability of Agri Loan?

Calculate profitability: (1) Total revenue = yield × price + government payments, (2) Total costs = variable costs (seed, fertilizer, pesticides, fuel, labor, irrigation) + fixed costs (land rent, depreciation, insurance, overhead), (3) Net profit = revenue − total costs, (4) Profit margin = (net profit ÷ revenue) × 100, (5) ROI = (net profit ÷ total investment) × 100. Break-even price = total costs ÷ yield. Break-even yield = total costs ÷ price. Positive net profit and ROI above cost of capital indicate profitability. Do enterprise analysis for each crop/enterprise separately to identify your best performers.

What costs should I include in Agri Loan budgeting?

Comprehensive Agri Loan budget includes: variable/operating costs (seed, fertilizer, lime, pesticides, fuel, repairs, hired labor, custom operations, irrigation, crop insurance, marketing), fixed/ownership costs (land rent/mortgage, property taxes, machinery depreciation and interest, building depreciation, insurance, utilities, overhead), and opportunity costs (value of your own labor and capital). For livestock: breeding stock, feed (purchased + raised), veterinary, breeding, bedding, marketing, mortality. Cash budgets track actual cash flow; full economic budgets include depreciation and opportunity costs for true profitability.

How do market prices affect Agri Loan decisions?

Market prices affect: planting decisions (high prices may incentivize more acreage, but the cobweb cycle often causes prices to fall when everyone plants more), input purchasing (forward-buy when prices are low), marketing timing (sell at harvest vs store — consider storage costs and price trends), hedging (use futures/options to lock prices), and enterprise selection (rotate to crops with favorable outlook). Price risk management tools: forward contracts, futures hedging, options, crop insurance, diversification. Never rely solely on current prices for long-term decisions — consider historical trends, cost of production, and outlook.

What is a good return on investment for Agri Loan?

Good ROI for Agri Loan depends on risk. For farming, target ROI of 10-20% on total assets is good (vs 5-10% for bonds). Return on equity (ROE) of 15-25% is strong. For individual investments (equipment, infrastructure), target payback of 3-7 years and ROI above cost of capital (5-8%). Some enterprises have higher ROI but higher risk (specialty crops, livestock); others have lower but stable ROI (grains). Calculate ROI per enterprise separately. Farming has inherent risks (weather, markets, disease) — higher expected returns should compensate for higher risk.

How can I improve the financial performance of Agri Loan?

Improve Agri Loan financial performance through: cost reduction (bulk input purchases, IPM to reduce pesticides, fuel efficiency, waste minimization), yield improvement (soil testing, proper fertilization, improved varieties, better pest management), price improvement (direct marketing, value-added products, premium markets, timing sales to avoid harvest gluts), risk management (crop insurance, diversification, hedging), efficiency improvement (precision agriculture, machinery matching), and financial management (regular budgeting, cash flow monitoring, debt management, tax planning). Even 5% yield increase + 3% cost reduction compounds to significant profit gains over time.

What are the FSA loan limits for 2026?

For 2026, FSA direct loan limits are: Direct Operating loans up to $400,000; Direct Ownership (farm ownership) loans up to $600,000; Microloans up to $50,000; and Down Payment loans for beginning farmers up to $300,150. The Guaranteed loan limit is $2,343,000 for FY2026 and is adjusted annually for inflation. If your loan amount exceeds a direct program limit, consider an FSA Guaranteed loan through a commercial lender.

What is the difference between a direct loan and a guaranteed loan?

Direct loans are made and serviced by the Farm Service Agency (FSA) itself, with interest rates set by FSA (for example, the October 2026 rates preset in this calculator). Guaranteed loans are made by commercial banks or other lenders with FSA guaranteeing repayment: the limit is higher ($2,343,000 for FY2026), but the interest rate is negotiated with your lender, which is why this calculator leaves the rate blank for the Guaranteed program and asks you to enter it manually.

What changed in FSA farm loan rules effective October 1, 2026?

Under the FSA final rule published September 4, 2026 and effective October 1, 2026: (1) the Application Fast Track (AFT) process is now permanent for qualifying direct loan borrowers — FSA uses financial benchmarks and repayment history to identify lower-risk applicants and streamline underwriting (a cash-flow budget is still required); emergency loans, youth loans, and loans tied to certain servicing actions are not eligible for AFT. (2) Crop insurance is now required: borrowers receiving direct or guaranteed FSA loans must obtain at least catastrophic (CAT) level coverage for crops when insurance is available — the old option to waive emergency crop-loss assistance instead of buying insurance was removed.

What are the October 2026 FSFL (Farm Storage Facility Loan) rates?

The October 2026 Commodity Credit Corporation (CCC) lending rates for Farm Storage Facility Loans (FSFL), published by FSA: 4.500% per annum for 3-year terms; 4.625% for 5-year terms; 4.750% for 7-year terms; 4.875% for 10-year terms; and 5.000% for 12-year terms. FSFL rates are fixed for the life of the loan and reset monthly — always check the FSA FSFL program page for the current month.
