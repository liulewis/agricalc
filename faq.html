Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our free agricultural calculators and farming tools.

Q: Are these agricultural calculators free to use?

A: Yes, all 40+ agricultural calculators on Agricalc are completely free to use. No registration, no subscription, no hidden fees. We generate revenue through affiliate links and advertisements, which keeps the tools free for everyone.

Q: How accurate are the farming calculators?

A: Our calculators use standard agricultural formulas and extension service recommendations. Results are accurate for planning purposes, but actual field results may vary due to soil conditions, weather, crop variety, and management practices. Always consult with local agronomists or extension services for site-specific advice.

Q: Can I use these calculators on my mobile phone?

A: Absolutely! All calculators are fully responsive and work on smartphones, tablets, and desktop computers. You can use them in the field while checking soil conditions, planning planting, or calculating fertilizer needs.

Q: Do you offer calculators for organic farming?

A: Yes, we have several calculators specifically for organic farming, including organic fertilizer calculator, cover crop seeding rates, compost application rates, and organic certification cost calculators. Many of our general calculators also work for organic systems when you input organic input values.

Q: How do I calculate seed rate for my farm?

A: Use our Seed Rate Calculator. Enter your field area, desired plant spacing (in-row and row spacing), thousand-grain weight of your seed variety, and germination rate. The calculator will tell you exactly how many kilograms of seed you need. For best results, use actual germination test results rather than bag label values.

Q: What calculators do you have for livestock farmers?

A: We offer 8 livestock calculators: feed calculator, FCR calculator, livestock density, aquaculture stocking, veterinary dosage, livestock weight estimator, beekeeping honey calculator, and mushroom substrate calculator.

Q: Can I save my calculations?

A: Yes! Our calculators include a history feature that saves your recent calculations in your browser. You can also bookmark specific calculator pages for quick access. We recommend writing down important results, as browser history may be cleared.

Q: Do you have calculators for greenhouse farming?

A: Yes, we have greenhouse-specific calculators including greenhouse ventilation calculator, greenhouse cost and payback calculator, and drip system designer. These help you design and manage efficient greenhouse operations.

Q: How often are calculators updated?

A: We regularly review and update our calculators based on the latest agricultural research, extension service recommendations, and user feedback. New calculators are added monthly. You can check our blog for updates and new tool announcements.

Q: Can I suggest a new calculator?

A: Absolutely! We welcome suggestions for new calculators. Contact us with your idea, and we'll consider adding it to our collection. We prioritize calculators that serve large groups of farmers and address common agricultural calculation needs.
