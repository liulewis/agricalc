October 2026 WASDE Prices: Corn $4.70, Soybeans $12.00, Wheat $6.30

10 Oct 2026 Agricalc Editorial Team 6 min read

Related Calculators: break-even price calculator · break-even yield calculator · farm budget calculator

Introduction

USDA released the October 2026 WASDE (WASDE-676) at noon ET on October 9. For 2026/27, the U.S. season-average farm price is projected at $4.70/bu for corn, $12.00 for soybeans and $6.30 for wheat. Corn and wheat are each $0.10 lower than September; soybeans are unchanged. This is market information from official USDA figures, not investment advice.

October vs September

The September baseline matches the official September report: corn $4.80, soybeans $12.00, wheat $6.40/bu.

Corn

Corn yield is raised to 181.2 bu/acre (+2.7) and production to 16.0 billion bushels. Ending stocks rise to 1.8 billion bushels, above the pre-report average expectation of 1.677 billion, so the price is lowered to $4.70/bu.

Soybeans

Soybean production is 4.56 billion bushels at 53.1 bu/acre. Ending stocks are 315 million bushels, near expectations, and the price is unchanged at $12.00/bu.

Wheat

U.S. wheat production is 1.534 billion bushels, the smallest since 1970, but ending stocks rise to 740 million bushels and the price is lowered to $6.30/bu. World ending stocks are 276.0 MMT.

Conclusion

October WASDE: corn $4.70, soybeans $12.00, wheat $6.30/bu for 2026/27. Use our break-even price calculator to test these benchmarks on your farm.
