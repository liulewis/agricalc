How to Use This Calculator

Select your crop type, enter the target yield in tonnes per hectare, set the previous crop N credit (kg N/ha) from your rotation, enter soil organic matter percentage, field area and local urea price. Click Calculate to see the net N rate and urea requirement.

About This Tool

This nitrogen rate calculator follows the standard extension-service approach: gross N requirement = crop N uptake per unit yield × target yield; net N = gross N − previous crop credit − soil organic matter mineralization. Urea at 46% N is the default N source.

Applicability: Applicable globally, with particular relevance for North American and European cropping systems. Adapt rates to your soil test results.

How the Calculation Works

Gross N = N per tonne yield × target yield (t/ha). Mineralized N from organic matter ≈ OM% × 12 kg N/ha (extension standard for temperate soils). Net N = gross N − previous crop credit − mineralized N. Urea required = net N ÷ 0.46 (urea is 46% N by weight).

Real-World Example

Maize targeting 8 t/ha needs 20 kg N/t → 160 kg N/ha gross. Previous soybean supplies 30 kg N/ha credit. Soil at 3% OM mineralizes ~36 kg N/ha. Net N to apply = 160 − 30 − 36 = 94 kg N/ha, requiring ~204 kg urea/ha (94 ÷ 0.46).

Common Mistakes & Tips

Common mistakes: ignoring legume cover crops in the rotation (soybean, alfalfa supply 30–100 kg N/ha free), applying all N at planting instead of splitting, overestimating mineralization on sandy cold soils, and using yield goals that are too optimistic. Always soil test nitrate in spring for pre-sidedress nitrate test (PSNT).

Frequently Asked Questions

How much nitrogen does corn need per hectare?

Corn needs about 20–25 kg N per tonne of grain yield. For a 10 t/ha target, gross N is 200–250 kg N/ha; after previous crop and organic matter credits, typical net N application is 150–200 kg N/ha, or 325–435 kg urea/ha.

Does soybeans really supply nitrogen to the next crop?

Yes. Grain soybean typically supplies 20–40 kg N/ha credit to the following crop; alfalfa and red clover can supply 80–150 kg N/ha. Always adjust the previous crop credit downward if the legume was poorly nodulated or was grazed/removed.

How much N does soil organic matter release per year?

Temperate mineral soils mineralize roughly 10–15 kg N/ha per 1% organic matter in the top 15 cm. This calculator uses 12 kg N/ha per 1% OM as a standard extension estimate. Sandy and cold soils mineralize less; warm, moist soils mineralize more.

Should I apply all nitrogen at once?

No. Split nitrogen applications reduce loss and improve efficiency. Apply 20–30% at planting as starter band, then top-dress the remainder at rapid growth (V4–V6 for corn, tillering for wheat). Side-dressing also allows adjusting N rate based on in-season yield expectations.

How do I convert kg N/ha to lb N/acre?

Multiply kg N/ha by 0.892 to get lb N/acre. Conversely, multiply lb N/acre by 1.12 to get kg N/ha. Urea at 46% N: 1 kg N/ha ÷ 0.46 = 2.17 kg urea/ha; 1 lb N/acre ÷ 0.46 = 2.17 lb urea/acre.
