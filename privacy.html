Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026. Your data stays in your browser — we never collect your calculator inputs.

Introduction

Agricalc ("we", "our", or "us") operates the agricalc.online website. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our website and services.

We take your privacy seriously. Our core principle is simple: your calculator inputs never leave your browser. All calculations are performed locally on your device using JavaScript, and we do not store, transmit, or collect any values you enter.

Information We Collect

We collect minimal information to operate and improve our services:

Usage Data

We use Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity to collect anonymousage statistics, including pages visited, time on site, browser type, device type, and approximate geographic location (country/region level). This data is not personally identifiable and is used solely to improve our website.

Calculator Inputs

We do NOT collect any calculator inputs. All calculations are performed locally in your browser using JavaScript. The numbers you enter (field area, fertilizer rates, livestock counts, etc.) never leave your device. Even if you use the share feature, the data is encoded in the URL you choose to share — we do not store it.

Favorites & History

Your favorite calculators and calculation history are stored locally in your browser using localStorage. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us. You can clear this datat any time through your browser settings.

Cookies

We use essential cookies for website functionality and analytics cookies for usage statistics. We do not use tracking cookies for advertising. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, though some site features may not work optimally.

PWA Offline Data

If you install our Progressive Web App, website files are cached locally on your device for offline use. This cache contains only static website files (HTML, CSS, JavaScript) — no personal data.

How We Use Your Information

  • To operate and maintain our website
  • To improve and optimize our calculators and content based on usage patterns
  • To understand how users interact with our site and which tools are most popular
  • To detect and preventechnical issues and errors
  • To ensure our website works correctly across different devices and browsers

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, orent your personal information. We may share anonymous, aggregated usage data with analytics providers (Google Analytics, Microsoft Clarity) solely for the purpose of understanding website usage patterns. These providers are contractually obligated to protect your data and use it only as directed by us.

Data Security

We implement appropriatechnical and organizational measures to protect your data. Our website uses HTTPS encryption for all traffic. However, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure.

Since our calculators run entirely in your browser, your input data never leaves your device — this the strongest possible privacy protection for your calculation data.

Third-Party Services

Our website may contain links to third-party websites (e.g., Amazon affiliate links, agricultural extension services, reference sources). We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of these third-party sites. Wencourage you to read the privacy policies of any third-party sites you visit.

Your Rights

You have the righto:

  • Access information we hold about you (we hold no personally identifiable information)
  • Request deletion of your data (you can clear browser cookies and localStorage at any time)
  • Opt out of analytics by using browser privacy settings, ad blockers, or the "Do Not Track" browser setting
  • Use our website without cookies (calculators will still work, though some preferences may not persist)

Children's Privacy

Our website is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will promptly delete it.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notifyou of any changes by posting the new policy on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Material changes will be highlighted on our homepage. Wencourage you to review this policy periodically.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, our data practices, or would like to exercise your privacy rights, please contact us through the website.
