Formula & How It Works

Elemental Sulfur (lb/acre) = pH Difference × Texture Coefficient

Texture coefficients (lb S per acre per pH point): Sandy = 300, Loam = 500, Clay = 800. Heavier soils buffer pH more strongly, requiring more sulfur. Elemental sulfur is oxidized by soil bacteria to sulfuric acid over 2-6 months.

Gypsum (tons/acre) = Sodium Difference × 1.5

Gypsum (CaSO₄·2H₂O) displaces exchangeable sodium with calcium, improving structure in sodic soils. The 1.5 factor accounts for purity and reaction efficiency.

Organic Matter (tons/acre) = OM Difference × 10

Only ~10-20% of applied organic matter persists long-term; the factor of 10 estimates the compost needed to raise soil OM by one percentage point in the top 6 inches.

Calculation Example

Sulfur: Loam soil, current pH 7.5, target 6.5 (1.0 point drop): Rate = 1.0 × 500 = 500 lb/acre. For 10 acres: 5,000 lb (2.5 tons). At $50/ton: $125.

Organic Matter: Current OM 2%, target 4% (2 points): Rate = 2 × 10 = 20 tons/acre. For 10 acres: 200 tons. At $50/ton: $10,000.

About This Tool

Soil amendments modify soil properties to improve crop growth. Elemental sulfur lowers pH in alkaline/calcareous soils, unlocking micronutrients like iron and zinc. Gypsum improves infiltration and reduces crusting in sodic and heavy clay soils without changing pH. Organic matter improves water-holding capacity, cation exchange capacity, and microbial activity. Always incorporate amendments into the root zone for best results.

Applicability: Designed for temperate and subtropical soils, applicable to farming systems across North America and Europe.

Common Mistakes & Tips

Common mistakes: Applying sulfur without retesting — over-application can overshoot pH targets and take months to reverse. Using gypsum to lower pH — gypsum does NOT change soil pH, it only improves structure. Expecting rapid OM increase — building soil organic matter takes years, not one application. Ignoring incorporation — surface-applied amendments move very slowly into the soil profile.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does elemental sulfur take to lower pH? ▼
Elemental sulfur must be oxidized by Thiobacillus bacteria to sulfuric acid. In warm, moist, well-aerated soils, this takes 2-4 months. In cool or dry conditions, it may take 6-12 months. Apply and incorporate 3-6 months before planting for best results.
Does gypsum lower soil pH? ▼
No. Gypsum (calcium sulfate) is a neutral salt and does not change soil pH. It improves soil structure by displacing sodium with calcium, which flocculates clay particles. Use elemental sulfur or aluminum sulfate to lower pH, and lime to raise pH.
How much can organic matter increase per application? ▼
A single 20-ton/acre compost application may raise topsoil OM by 0.5-1.5 percentage points initially, but much of this decomposes within 1-2 years. Sustained increases of 1% OM require repeated applications over 3-5 years combined with reduced tillage and cover cropping.
What units does the Soil Amendment Calculator use? ▼
Current/target values unitless, area in acres, amendment type selectable, soil texture selectable, price in $/ton. Results in lb/acre or tons/acre and total cost.
How do I use the Soil Amendment Calculator correctly? ▼
Start by gathering accurate input values from your farm records or measurements. Enter each value in the specified units, then click Calculate. Review all results carefully — cross-check the primary output against your own experience or extension recommendations. For best results, use actual measured values rather than estimates, and recalibrate whenever conditions change.
