Seed Treatment Calculator

Compute the exact chemical volume needed to treat a seed lot.

Enter the seed weight, the label rate (fl oz per cwt / 100 lb), and the product concentration. The calculator converts seed weight to hundredweight, multiplies by the label rate, and gives chemical volume in fl oz and mL, plus pure active ingredient.

A small ag dealer treated 5,000 lb of wheat at 8 fl oz/cwt but measured 8 fl oz per 1,000 lb instead of per 100 lb — a tenfold under-dose that left the crop vulnerable to wireworms. The label rate is always per hundredweight.

How the Calculation Works

Seed in hundredweight (cwt) = seed lb ÷ 100. Chemical volume (fl oz) = cwt × label rate (fl oz/cwt). mL = fl oz × 29.57. Active ingredient (fl oz) = chemical volume × concentration ÷ 100.

Real-World Example

5,000 lb of seed = 50 cwt. At 8 fl oz/cwt: 50 × 8 = 400 fl oz ≈ 11,830 mL. At 50% concentration: 200 fl oz active ingredient.

Common Mistakes & Tips

Common mistakes: reading the label rate per 1,000 lb instead of per cwt (100 lb); mixing up fl oz with fluid pounds; treating unevenly in the drum; ignoring slurry volume for application. Always follow the label — it is the legal document.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a cwt?

A hundredweight (cwt) is 100 lb in the US. Seed treatment label rates are written per cwt, so divide your total seed weight in pounds by 100 before multiplying by the rate.

Why does concentration matter?

Two products with the same fl oz rate can contain different amounts of active ingredient. Concentration tells you the fraction that actually controls pests; the rest is carrier and inert ingredients.

How do I apply the treatment evenly?

Use a calibrated seed treater or drum. Add the chemical slowly while tumbling the seed, and weigh output from the treater to confirm the target rate is achieved. Wear required PPE.

Can I mix two seed treatments?

Only if the label allows tank-mixing. Some combinations are incompatible and can clog equipment or reduce efficacy. When in doubt, contact the manufacturer.

How do I convert fl oz to mL?

1 US fluid ounce = 29.57 mL. If your equipment is calibrated in mL, multiply fl oz by 29.57. The calculator does this for you.
