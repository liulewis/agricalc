About Agricalc

Empowering farmers worldwide with free, science-based agricultural tools and knowledge.

Our Mission

Agricalc was builto make agricultural planning accessible to everyone. Whether you are a small-scale gardener, a commercial farmer, an agronomist, or an agricultural student, our tools help you make data-driven decisions — without needing expensive software or complex spreadsheets.

We believe that good farming starts with good numbers. Accurate seed rates, fertilizer calculations, irrigation scheduling, carbon footprint tracking, and feed rations can mean the difference between a profitable season and a loss. Our goal is to put these calculations at your fingertips — free, fast, and no registration required.

134 Specialized Calculators

Covering planting, soil & land management, livestock & aquaculture, carbon footprint, beekeeping, mushroom farming, and general farm management. Each calculator is built with agricultural science formulas and reference data.

In-Depth Guides

Every calculator supports both metric and imperial units. Switch between hectares/acres, kg/lbs, Celsius/Fahrenheit, and more with a single click — values convert automatically.

Save your most-used calculators to favorites for quick access. Automatically track your calculation history so you can review and comparesults over time — all stored locally in your browser.

Our Approach

Science-Based Formulas

Every calculator use established agricultural formulas from university extension services, agricultural textbooks, and industry standards. We cite reference ranges for common crops and livestock so you can verify our assumptions against your local conditions.

Transparency

We show the formulas behind every calculation. No black boxes. You can see exactly how we arrive at each number, and adjust inputs to match your specific situation. Each calculator includes a detailed explanation of the methodology.

Continuous Improvement

Agriculture is constantly evolving. We regularly update our calculators and guides to reflect new research, changing best practices, and user feedback. We recently added carbon footprint tracking, beekeeping calculators, mushroom farming tools, and multi-language support.
