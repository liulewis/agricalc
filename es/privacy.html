Política de Privacidad

Data Collection

Agricalc is a static website. All calculations are performed locally in your browser. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data or calculation inputs tour servers.

Local Storage

Our tools use browser localStorage to provide optional features: favorites, calculation history, and unit preferences. This data stays on your device and is never sento us. You can clear this datat any time through your browser settings.

Cookies

We do not use tracking cookies. The only storage used is the localStorage mentioned above for user convenience features.

Third-Party Services

Our website may contain links to third-party websites (such as Amazon). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of thesexternal sites.

PWA & Offline Data

Our Progressive Web App uses a service worker to cache files for offline use. This caching is done locally on your device and does not involve data collection.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please contact us through our website.
