Acerca de Agricalc

Agricalc es una colección de 40+ calculadoras agrícolas online gratuitas diseñadas para ayudar agricultores, agrónomos y estudiantes a tomar decisiones basadas en datos.

Our Mission

We believery farmer deserves access to professional-grade agricultural tools. Our calculators bring agronomic science to your fingertips — completely free, no registration required.

What We Offer

  • 134 Specialized Calculators — covering planting, land & soil, livestock, and general farm management
  • 12 In-Depth Guides — science-based articles on soil testing, irrigation, fertilization, and more
  • 3 Languages — English, Spanish, and French for global farmers
  • 53+ Agricultural Terms — comprehensive glossary ofarming terminology
  • Unit Switching — toggle between metric and imperial systems
  • Save & Share — bookmark favorites, view calculation history, and export results

Who Is This For?

  • Commercial farmers and ranchers
  • Agricultural students and researchers
  • Agronomists and farm consultants
  • Home gardeners and small-scale growers
  • Anyone interested in data-driven farming

Our Commitment

All calculations are based on established agronomic formulas and peer-reviewed research. We continuously update our tools to reflect best practices. However, our calculators are for reference only — always consult local agricultural experts for site-specific advice.

Revisado por el Equipo Editorial de Agricalc — Ingenieros agrícolas y agrónomos con más de 10 años de experiencia en campo. Nuestro equipo verifica cada fórmula contra publicaciones de extensión revisadas por pares. Para asesoría específica, consulte a su agrónomo local.
